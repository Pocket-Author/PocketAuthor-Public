## Editorial standard

Every full-copy piece should:

- start with a real reader tension rather than a generic product claim
- make a complete argument
- include concrete examples or practical application
- distinguish useful assistance from guarantees
- keep rights, provenance, and author approval visible where relevant
- end with one clear PocketAuthor next step
- be useful even if the reader never creates an account

## Claims guardrails

Do not claim:

- guaranteed publication
- guaranteed retailer acceptance
- guaranteed sales
- bestseller status
- guaranteed media coverage
- guaranteed professional authority
- invented customer outcomes

Use consent and context for customer stories. Label assumptions. Link changing third-party requirements back to their official sources.
