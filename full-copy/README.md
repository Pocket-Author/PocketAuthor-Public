Use these pieces as source material for:

- landing pages
- Substack/newsletter essays
- LinkedIn long-form posts
- sales collateral
- workshop descriptions
- founder-led marketing
- content repurposing
- SEO pages

## Copy principles

These pieces should stay aligned with the public PocketAuthor claims policy:

- no guaranteed publication
- no guaranteed retailer acceptance
- no guaranteed sales
- no fake bestseller claims
- no invented testimonials
- no fabricated research
- customer stories require consent and context
- assumptions should be labeled
- sources should be traceable
- authors approve meaningful public uses of their work
