# Word Processor vs. Book Workspace

A word processor is excellent at documents. A book workspace treats the book as a project with chapters, sources, revisions, metadata, collaborators, and editions.

## The practical difference

Use a simple document when the manuscript is the only thing you need to manage. Use a book workspace when the surrounding workflow is becoming the hard part.

## Compare the decision on five dimensions

### 1. Primary outcome
What are you actually trying to produce: text, a finished book, a publishing path, professional feedback, or audience growth?

### 2. Human judgment
Which decisions require expertise, taste, accountability, or personal experience?

### 3. Workflow complexity
How many sources, chapters, collaborators, revisions, and outputs are involved?

### 4. Control and ownership
Who controls the manuscript, rights, files, deadlines, and publishing accounts?

### 5. Cost of coordination
How much time is being lost moving material between tools and people?
