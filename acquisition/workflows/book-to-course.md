# How to Turn a Book Into a Course

A book can become the foundation of a course—but chapters should not simply become videos.

A course needs learning outcomes, practice and feedback.

## Workflow

1. Define what learners should be able to do.
2. Map chapters to learning outcomes.
3. Group related chapters into modules.
4. Extract frameworks and exercises.
5. Create assignments.
6. Add examples.
7. Decide what needs video or live explanation.
8. Build worksheets.
9. Create assessment or reflection points.
10. Keep the book as the durable reference source.

## Why this works

The book provides depth.

The course provides guided practice.
