# How to Turn Existing Notes Into a Book

You do not need organized notes to start.

You need a way to discover what the notes are trying to become.

## Your notes may contain

- observations
- quotes
- frameworks
- half-written essays
- meeting notes
- research
- questions
- personal stories
- links
- voice memos

## Workflow

1. Gather notes into one source inventory.
2. Tag recurring ideas.
3. Identify the ideas with the most evidence and energy.
4. Choose the reader.
5. Write a one-sentence book promise.
6. Create a rough chapter spine.
7. Attach relevant notes to chapters.
8. Identify research gaps.
9. Draft from the strongest source clusters.
10. Delete notes that do not serve the book.
