# How to Validate a Book Idea

A book idea needs more than enthusiasm.

It needs enough substance to sustain a useful reader journey.

## Test the idea

Ask:

- Who is the reader?
- What problem does the book solve?
- What changes after reading?
- What does the author know that is distinctive?
- Is there enough source material?
- Is there enough evidence?
- Can the idea support multiple distinct chapters?
- Does the book have a reason to exist now?
- Is the scope too broad?

## Strong signal

The author can explain the book in one sentence without using vague words like “everything,” “life,” or “success.”
