# How to Prepare a Book for Bookstores

Bookstore availability is not automatic.

A professional approach starts with a book that is easy to understand, order and evaluate.

## Prepare

- finished edition
- professional cover
- clear metadata
- ISBN
- price
- distribution availability
- return/discount terms where relevant
- author information
- book description
- marketing plan

## Research stores

Look for:

- genre fit
- local interest
- event programs
- existing comparable titles
- submission or stocking policies
