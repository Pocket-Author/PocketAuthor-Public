# How to Choose the Right Book Type

The format of the book should follow what the reader needs.

## Examples

Choose a:

- **field guide** when the reader needs practical navigation
- **workbook** when the reader needs exercises
- **manifesto** when the goal is a strong argument
- **handbook** when the reader needs reference material
- **memoir** when lived experience drives the meaning
- **business book** when a framework or market insight is central
- **textbook** when learning sequence and instruction matter

## Questions

- What should the reader understand?
- What should the reader do?
- How often will they return to the book?
- Does the book need exercises?
- Is story or reference more important?
- Is the author teaching a method or making an argument?
