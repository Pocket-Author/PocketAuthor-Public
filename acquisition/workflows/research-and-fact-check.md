# How to Research and Fact-Check a Nonfiction Book

Strong nonfiction makes claims traceable.

## Research workflow

1. List the major claims.
2. Identify which claims need sources.
3. Prefer primary sources where practical.
4. Track publication dates.
5. Record exact quotation locations.
6. Separate fact from interpretation.
7. Flag disputed claims.
8. Review outdated evidence.
9. Verify names, dates and numbers.
10. Re-check high-risk claims before publication.

## High-risk topics

Pay extra attention to:

- law
- medicine
- science
- finance
- statistics
- history
- current events
- named people
- quotations
