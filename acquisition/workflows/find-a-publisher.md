# How to Find a Publisher for Your Book

The goal is not to find the largest publisher.

The goal is to find publishers that fit your book.

## Before searching

Know:

- genre
- audience
- book type
- comparable titles
- manuscript status
- whether you need an agent
- territory
- rights you are willing to discuss

## Research publishers

Look for:

- books similar to yours
- current catalog
- country or market focus
- submission rules
- agent requirements
- genre focus
- rights information
- recent releases

## Build a shortlist

A good shortlist is smaller and more relevant than a massive directory export.

Track:

- publisher
- imprint
- contact/submission page
- fit
- submission requirements
- status
- date submitted
- response
