# How to Build a Book Team

Not every book needs every professional.

The right team depends on the manuscript, publishing route, budget and goals.

## Common roles

- developmental editor
- line editor
- copy editor
- proofreader
- researcher
- fact checker
- ghostwriter
- translator
- sensitivity or subject expert
- cover designer
- interior designer
- literary agent
- publicist
- marketer
- audiobook producer

## How to decide who you need

Ask:

1. What work can the author do well?
2. What requires specialist judgment?
3. What must be independently reviewed?
4. What is required by the publishing route?
5. Where is the highest risk?

## Define the brief

For each professional specify:

- scope
- inputs
- deliverables
- deadline
- revision rounds
- access
- approval
- finish line
