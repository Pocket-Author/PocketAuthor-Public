# How to Turn a Book Into a Business Asset

A book can be valuable even when direct book revenue is not the primary goal.

## A book can support

- speaking
- consulting
- workshops
- corporate training
- courses
- community
- client education
- partnerships
- media
- bulk sales
- licensing
- next title

## Workflow

1. Identify the book’s strongest frameworks.
2. Turn chapters into teaching modules.
3. Create a workshop.
4. Create short-form educational content.
5. Build a speaking topic.
6. Create a lead resource.
7. Package bulk-sales offers.
8. Track which opportunities come from the book.
9. Update the book from new learning.
10. Build the next title from the expanded body of work.
