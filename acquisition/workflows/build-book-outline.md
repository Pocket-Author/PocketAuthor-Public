# How to Build a Book Outline

A book outline should describe the reader’s journey—not just list topics.

## Start with

- reader
- promise
- thesis
- starting point
- desired outcome

## For every chapter define

- purpose
- reader question
- central claim
- evidence
- example/story
- practical takeaway
- transition

## Test the order

Ask:

- Does the reader need chapter 2 before chapter 3?
- Does each chapter earn its place?
- Are two chapters repeating the same job?
- Are there missing research sections?
- Does the ending deliver the promise?
