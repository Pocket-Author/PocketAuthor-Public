# How to Find Book Grants and Writing Funding

A book can require time, travel, research, translation, editing or production support.

Grants and residencies can sometimes help.

## Types of opportunities

- writing grants
- literary grants
- research grants
- translation grants
- residencies
- fellowships
- project funding
- publication support
- travel support

## Before applying

Check:

- eligibility
- geography
- genre
- career stage
- project stage
- required budget
- deadline
- application fee
- rights or reporting requirements

## Build an application system

Track:

- opportunity
- fit
- deadline
- requirements
- work sample
- proposal
- references
- budget
- status
- result
