# How to Turn a Course Into a Book

A course and a book can share knowledge, but they are experienced differently.

Courses can depend on live explanation, assignments and instructor context.

Books have to carry more of the learning journey themselves.

## Start with

- syllabus
- lecture notes
- slides
- exercises
- student questions
- readings
- examples
- assessments

## Workflow

1. Define the book reader.
2. Decide whether the book is a textbook, workbook, guide or companion.
3. Convert modules into chapter logic.
4. Add explanations that were previously delivered live.
5. Turn exercises into book-friendly activities.
6. Add examples and references.
7. Remove course-management material.
8. Review accessibility.
9. Edit for independent reading.
10. Prepare companion resources separately.
