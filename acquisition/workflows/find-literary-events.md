# How to Find Literary Events for Authors

Literary events can help authors learn, meet readers, understand publishing and build relationships.

## Event types

- book fairs
- writing conferences
- author talks
- workshops
- poetry events
- publishing conferences
- festivals
- book clubs
- online events

## Choose events based on your goal

### Learn
Look for craft and publishing workshops.

### Meet industry professionals
Look for publishing conferences and book fairs.

### Meet readers
Look for festivals, bookstores and community events.

### Speak
Look for topic-aligned conferences and organizations.
