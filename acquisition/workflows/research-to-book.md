# How to Turn Research Into a Book

A research archive is not automatically a reader-friendly book.

The challenge is turning evidence into a clear public argument.

## Workflow

1. Define the reader.
2. Define the central claim.
3. Separate primary from secondary evidence.
4. Create a source map.
5. Identify the minimum background the reader needs.
6. Build chapters around questions rather than paper sections.
7. Translate technical language carefully.
8. Use stories or examples where appropriate.
9. Preserve citations and uncertainty.
10. Fact-check every major claim before publication.

## Good research books do not hide complexity

They explain it.
