# How to Turn Consulting Frameworks Into a Book

Consulting frameworks can become strong books because they already contain a repeated way of seeing a problem.

## Start with the framework

Document:

- the problem it solves
- the situations where it works
- the situations where it fails
- the steps or components
- the evidence behind it
- common client mistakes
- examples and counterexamples

## Turn the framework into a reader journey

1. Explain the problem.
2. Show why existing approaches fail.
3. Introduce the framework.
4. Explain each component.
5. Show how the pieces interact.
6. Add examples.
7. Address objections.
8. Give the reader a practical implementation path.

## Protect client confidentiality

Use consented or anonymized examples and avoid revealing proprietary client information.
