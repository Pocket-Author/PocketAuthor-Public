# How to Turn a Thesis or Dissertation Into a Book

PocketAuthor's core source-material idea is simple:

**you may already have more of the book than you think.**

The challenge is turning material created for another purpose into a coherent reader journey.

## What you may already have

- dissertation chapters
- literature review
- datasets
- interviews
- appendices

## A practical workflow

1. Choose the new reader.
2. Rewrite the central argument for that reader.
3. Reduce literature-review material that only served examination requirements.
4. Move methodology detail to the level the reader actually needs.
5. Rebuild chapters around questions and consequences.
6. Add narrative context and examples.
7. Update the evidence.
8. Check permissions for previously published material.
9. Rewrite the introduction and conclusion completely.
10. Edit for a book voice rather than a defense voice.

## Common mistake

**A dissertation is written for examiners; a book is written for readers.**

The new book needs its own promise, structure, evidence, voice, and editorial logic.

## Questions to ask before drafting

- Who is this book for?
- What should the reader understand or do afterward?
- What material is uniquely yours?
- What material needs permission?
- Which claims need updated evidence?
- Which parts repeat each other?
- What is missing from the archive?
- Which content was created for a different audience or platform?
- What should be left out?
