# How to Turn Research Papers Into a Book

Research papers are optimized for scholarly communication. A book needs a broader reader journey.

## What changes

A public-facing book usually needs:

- more context
- less disciplinary shorthand
- clearer stakes
- stronger narrative progression
- examples
- stories
- synthesis across papers
- explicit uncertainty
- updated evidence

## Workflow

1. Identify the unifying public question.
2. Choose the reader.
3. Map papers to themes.
4. Remove duplicated literature review material.
5. Build a new chapter architecture.
6. Explain methods only to the level readers need.
7. Update older evidence.
8. Preserve citations.
9. Add examples and implications.
10. Rewrite the introduction and conclusion around the book, not the publication history.
