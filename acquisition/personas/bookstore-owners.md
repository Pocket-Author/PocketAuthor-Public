# PocketAuthor for Bookstore Owners

## Bookstores are literary infrastructure

Independent bookstores do more than sell books.

They host:

- author events
- book clubs
- community conversations
- school relationships
- local discovery
- reading culture

## PocketAuthor public ecosystem

Bookstore owners can connect into:

- bookstore directory
- literary events
- book clubs
- organizations
