# PocketAuthor for Memoir and Legacy Authors

## Preserve lived experience without turning your life into a timeline dump

A memoir needs meaning, not just chronology.

## Source material

- journals
- letters
- photographs
- family stories
- recorded interviews
- documents
- personal notes
- historical context

## Workflow

1. Decide what the book is really about.
2. Identify the emotional arc.
3. Choose which memories belong.
4. Interview yourself and others.
5. Verify important historical facts.
6. Separate private material from publishable material.
7. Build chapters around meaning, not only dates.
8. Review names, permissions and sensitivities.
9. Edit for voice.
10. Prepare the edition.
