# PocketAuthor for Creators

## Turn years of content into one coherent book

Creators often have no shortage of material.

The problem is structure.

Your archive may include:

- podcast episodes
- newsletters
- videos
- livestreams
- essays
- threads
- interviews
- audience questions

The book should not simply copy those fragments.

It should discover the deeper thesis beneath them.

## Workflow

1. Gather your strongest material.
2. Cluster recurring themes.
3. Identify the argument.
4. Remove repetition.
5. Add missing research.
6. Build chapter progression.
7. Preserve your voice.
8. Draft and revise.
9. Turn the book back into derivative content.
