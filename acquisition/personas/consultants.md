# PocketAuthor for Consultants

## Turn your methodology into a book clients can understand before they hire you

Consultants often already have:

- frameworks
- diagnostics
- workshop decks
- client education
- repeatable deliverables
- case patterns
- operating models
- common client questions

A book can turn those into a durable, teachable system.

## What a consultant book can do

- explain your methodology
- build authority
- educate prospects
- support speaking
- create workshops
- shorten sales cycles
- turn expertise into reusable IP

## Good book formats

- practical guide
- field guide
- framework book
- workbook
- playbook
- industry handbook

## Workflow

1. Identify the repeated client problem.
2. Define your method.
3. Separate proprietary client information from general lessons.
4. Turn the method into chapters.
5. Add stories and evidence.
6. Build exercises or tools.
7. Edit for clarity.
8. Publish.
9. Reuse chapters as workshops, newsletters and course modules.
