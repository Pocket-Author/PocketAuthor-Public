# PocketAuthor for Ghostwriters

## Manage the engagement around the manuscript, not just the manuscript itself

Ghostwriting involves much more than drafting.

A professional engagement may include:

- voice capture
- interviews
- research
- source material
- claim review
- story structure
- approvals
- revisions
- production handoff

## Workflow

1. Build the author voice profile.
2. Plan interviews.
3. Collect source material.
4. Build the book architecture.
5. Track claims and evidence.
6. Draft.
7. Route approvals.
8. Manage revision stages.
9. Prepare production handoff.
10. Preserve project history.
