# PocketAuthor for Editors

## Give every editorial stage a clear job and finish line

Editing a book is not one task.

It may involve:

- developmental editing
- structural editing
- line editing
- copy editing
- fact checking
- proofreading

## What editors need

- stable manuscript versions
- comments
- revision history
- source context
- author decisions
- chapter status
- handoff clarity

## PocketAuthor narrative

The product should help editors see:

- what stage the manuscript is in
- what changed
- what still needs review
- what evidence supports the claim
- what the author approved
- what is ready for production
