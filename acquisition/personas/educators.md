# PocketAuthor for Educators

## Turn years of teaching into a durable learning resource

Educators already create books in fragments:

- lectures
- slides
- assignments
- exercises
- syllabi
- course notes
- rubrics
- FAQs

PocketAuthor can help turn that material into a textbook, workbook, field guide or course companion.

## Workflow

1. Define learning outcomes.
2. Choose the book format.
3. Organize lessons into chapter progression.
4. Add examples and exercises.
5. Build review questions.
6. Add references.
7. Review accessibility.
8. Prepare companion resources.
9. Publish.
10. Turn the book into course assets.
