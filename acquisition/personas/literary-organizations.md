# PocketAuthor for Literary Organizations

## Make the ecosystem around books easier to discover

Literary organizations include:

- presses
- festivals
- writing centers
- libraries
- nonprofits
- bookstores
- reading groups
- literary communities

## What public discovery can support

- organizational profiles
- events
- programs
- relationships
- geographic discovery
- source-backed information
