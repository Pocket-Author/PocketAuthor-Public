# PocketAuthor for Researchers

## Turn rigorous research into a book a broader audience can understand

Research books need two things at once:

**credibility and readability.**

## Existing material

- papers
- datasets
- field notes
- interviews
- literature reviews
- archival sources
- conference talks
- unpublished analysis

## Workflow

1. Identify the public argument.
2. Decide what evidence is essential.
3. Separate primary and secondary sources.
4. Build the reader journey.
5. Translate technical language carefully.
6. Preserve citations and provenance.
7. Flag uncertainty.
8. Add examples and stories.
9. Fact-check the manuscript.
10. Prepare references and permissions.
