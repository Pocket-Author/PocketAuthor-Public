# How to Write a Decision Book

A decision book helps readers make a recurring high-stakes choice better.

## Organize around decisions

For each chapter, define:

- the decision
- the information available
- the tradeoffs
- common errors
- the framework
- examples
- edge cases

## Strong decision books teach judgment

They do not only tell readers what to do.

They explain how to think when conditions change.
