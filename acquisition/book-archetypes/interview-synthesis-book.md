# How to Write an Interview-Synthesis Book

An interview-synthesis book combines many voices to answer one larger question.

## The author still has a job

The manuscript needs:

- a clear research question
- consistent interview method
- themes
- contrasting perspectives
- source context
- synthesis
- verification

## Avoid transcript dumping

Use interviews as evidence and perspective inside an authored structure.
