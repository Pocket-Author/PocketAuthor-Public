# How to Write a Category-Creation Book

A category-creation book argues that people are naming, measuring, or solving a problem the wrong way—and proposes a better frame.

## Core structure

1. The old category
2. Why it no longer explains reality
3. The new category
4. The evidence behind it
5. What changes when the category changes
6. Examples
7. Objections
8. The practical implications

## What makes this credible

A category book needs more than branding language.

Use:
- evidence
- market history
- clear definitions
- counterexamples
- practical consequences
