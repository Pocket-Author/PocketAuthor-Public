# How to Write a Professional Memoir

A professional memoir uses a career as the narrative spine while focusing on the ideas, decisions, and lessons that matter to readers.

## It is not a résumé in prose

Select:

- turning points
- failures
- changed beliefs
- difficult decisions
- industry shifts
- mentors
- moments that reveal a larger lesson

## Balance story and usefulness

The reader should leave with more than a chronology.

They should understand what the experience means.
