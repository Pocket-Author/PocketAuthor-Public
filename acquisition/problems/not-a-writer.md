# I’m Not a Writer, but I Want to Write a Book

You do not need to identify as a writer before you can become an author.

## A practical answer

Start from what you know, speak ideas aloud if that is easier, organize source material, and use editors or collaborators where professional help improves the book.

## Work through these questions

- What is the exact reader problem?
- What information is fact versus memory or opinion?
- What source material already exists?
- Which decisions require professional judgment?
- What should be documented now so it is not reconstructed later?
- What can be simplified without becoming misleading?

## Useful workflow

1. Define the reader and goal.
2. Inventory the source material.
3. Make the next decision explicit.
4. Attach evidence where needed.
5. Separate drafting from verification.
6. Review with the right professional when the risk is high.
7. Keep a record of what changed and why.
