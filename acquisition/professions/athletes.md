# Book Ideas for Athletes

## What should athletes write a book about?

A strong professional book begins with **author advantage**: repeated experience, distinctive judgment, evidence, and source material that readers cannot easily get elsewhere.

## Five directions to test

1. **What High Performance Actually Requires** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
2. **The Mental Systems Behind Consistency** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
3. **A Career Beyond the Highlight Reel** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
4. **Lessons From Injury and Recovery** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
5. **How Elite Preparation Changes Decision-Making** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.

## Source material already hiding in the work

- training journals
- public interviews
- competition notes
- coaching feedback
- personal reflections

## Questions that reveal the book

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What took years to learn?
- What do people repeatedly ask you to explain?
- Which mistakes keep recurring?
- What changed your mind?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after the reader finishes?

## What to protect

Protect medical privacy, team confidentiality, and third-party stories that require consent.
