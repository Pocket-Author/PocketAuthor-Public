# Book Ideas for Project Managers

## What should project managers write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **How Complex Work Actually Gets Delivered** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Project Tradeoffs** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **Why Plans Fail and Teams Recover** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **The Decisions Behind Reliable Execution** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **How to Manage Dependencies Without Losing the Goal** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- retrospectives
- project plans
- risk logs
- training material
- delivery frameworks

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Remove confidential project, client, personnel, and vendor information.
