# Book Ideas for Marketers

## What should marketers write a book about?

A strong professional book begins with **author advantage**: repeated experience, distinctive judgment, evidence, and source material that readers cannot easily get elsewhere.

## Five directions to test

1. **How Markets Actually Learn a New Category** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
2. **A Field Guide to Message-Market Fit** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
3. **What Attribution Cannot Tell You** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
4. **Lessons From Campaigns That Changed Strategy** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.
5. **How to Build a Narrative That Compounds** — validate it against the author’s real experience, audience, and evidence.

## Source material already hiding in the work

- campaign retrospectives
- positioning docs
- research
- creative briefs
- public case studies

## Questions that reveal the book

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What took years to learn?
- What do people repeatedly ask you to explain?
- Which mistakes keep recurring?
- What changed your mind?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after the reader finishes?

## What to protect

Protect client data, unpublished performance data, and confidential campaign strategy.
