# Book Ideas for Therapists

## What should therapists write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **What People Misunderstand About Change** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Emotional Skills** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **How to Think About Patterns Without Pathologizing People** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **What Therapy Can and Cannot Do** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **A Practical Guide to Better Self-Reflection** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- psychoeducation
- public talks
- research
- generalized clinical patterns
- exercises

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect client confidentiality and clearly distinguish education from diagnosis or individualized mental-health advice.
