# Book Ideas for Sports Coaches

## What should sports coaches write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **How Great Teams Learn** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Practice Design** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **What Athletes Need From Feedback** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **Lessons From Seasons That Changed the Program** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **How Culture Becomes Performance** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- practice plans
- public talks
- coaching notes
- team principles
- research

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect athlete privacy, medical information, minors, and confidential team strategy.
