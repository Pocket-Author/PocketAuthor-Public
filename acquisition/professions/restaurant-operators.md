# Book Ideas for Restaurant Operators

## What should restaurant operators write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **The Operating System Behind a Great Restaurant** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **What Guests Never See About Hospitality** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **A Field Guide to Service Recovery** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **How Kitchens and Front-of-House Learn to Work Together** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **Lessons From Opening and Scaling Restaurants** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- SOPs
- menus
- training
- service notes
- supplier/process lessons

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect staff privacy, vendor pricing, recipes or processes treated as proprietary, and confidential business information.
