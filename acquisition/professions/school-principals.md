# Book Ideas for School Principals

## What should school principals write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **What School Leadership Looks Like Beyond Policy** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Building School Culture** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **How Leaders Make Decisions With Limited Resources** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **Lessons From Family and Community Trust** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **What New Principals Learn Too Late** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- school improvement plans
- public policies
- staff training
- leadership notes
- community engagement lessons

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect student, family, and staff privacy and distinguish local policy from universal practice.
