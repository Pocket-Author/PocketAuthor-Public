# Book Ideas for Sales Leaders

## What should sales leaders write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **What Great Sales Teams Actually Do Differently** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Complex Deals** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **How Buyers Really Make Decisions** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **Lessons From Lost Deals** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **How to Build a Repeatable Sales System** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- sales training
- public win/loss lessons
- call frameworks
- playbooks
- market research

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect customer information, pricing, pipeline details, and confidential commercial terms.
