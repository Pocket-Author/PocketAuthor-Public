# Book Ideas for UX Researchers

## What should ux researchers write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **How to Learn What Users Actually Need** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Better Research Questions** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **What Product Teams Misread About Evidence** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **How to Synthesize Messy Qualitative Data** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **Lessons From Research That Changed the Roadmap** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- research plans
- public case studies
- interview guides
- synthesis frameworks
- workshop material

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect participant privacy, client/company confidentiality, and proprietary research.
