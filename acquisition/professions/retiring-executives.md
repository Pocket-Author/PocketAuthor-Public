# Book Ideas for Retiring Executives

## What should retiring executives write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **What I Wish I Understood Earlier** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **The Decisions That Defined a Career** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **A Field Guide for the Next Generation of Leaders** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **How the Industry Changed—and What Still Matters** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **The Lessons Hidden Behind the Job Titles** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- speeches
- memos
- board materials that can be shared
- career notes
- interviews

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Respect board, employee, client, deal, and company confidentiality and check post-employment obligations.
