# Book Ideas for Photographers

## What should photographers write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **How to Build a Visual Story With Meaning** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Documentary Practice** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **What Photographers Learn About Attention** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **The Ethics Behind Image-Making** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **Lessons From Long-Term Projects** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- photo essays
- project notes
- captions
- public exhibitions
- interviews

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Respect model releases, image licensing, privacy, and ownership of collaborative or commissioned work.
