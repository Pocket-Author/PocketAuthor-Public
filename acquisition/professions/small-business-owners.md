# Book Ideas for Small Business Owners

## What should small business owners write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **The Playbook Behind a Durable Small Business** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **What Customers Taught Me About the Market** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **A Field Guide to Local Operations** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **Lessons From Surviving Difficult Years** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **How to Build a Business That Works Without Constant Heroics** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- SOPs
- customer FAQs
- training
- financial lessons that can be generalized
- owner journals

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect customer, employee, financial, and vendor confidentiality and avoid unsupported business-result claims.
