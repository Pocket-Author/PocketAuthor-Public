# Book Ideas for Procurement Leaders

## What should procurement leaders write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **How Procurement Actually Creates Value** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Supplier Decisions** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **What Buyers Get Wrong About Total Cost** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **Lessons From Failed Vendor Relationships** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **How to Build Better Sourcing Systems** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- sourcing frameworks
- public procurement guidance
- supplier evaluation templates
- training
- case studies

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect vendor pricing, bids, confidential negotiations, and regulated procurement information.
