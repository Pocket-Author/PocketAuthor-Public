# Book Ideas for Recruiters

## What should recruiters write a book about?

A strong professional book turns repeated experience into a useful framework for a specific reader.

## Five directions to test

1. **What Hiring Managers Misunderstand About Talent** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
2. **A Field Guide to Better Candidate Decisions** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
3. **Why Hiring Processes Fail** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
4. **How to Evaluate Potential With Better Evidence** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.
5. **Lessons From Thousands of Candidate Conversations** — validate it against your real experience, evidence, and audience.

## Source material you may already have

- interview frameworks
- training
- public hiring research
- candidate question patterns
- process retrospectives

## Questions that reveal the strongest direction

- What do outsiders misunderstand?
- What do beginners repeatedly get wrong?
- What problem have you solved many times?
- What changed your mind?
- Which lesson took years to learn?
- What can you support with evidence?
- Which stories illustrate the lesson?
- Who needs this knowledge most?
- What should change after reading?

## What to protect

Protect candidate privacy and avoid revealing confidential hiring or compensation information.
