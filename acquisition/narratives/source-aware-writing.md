# Source-Aware Writing

A source-aware manuscript remembers where important ideas, facts, quotes, and evidence came from.

## What source awareness can support

- citations
- fact checking
- quotation review
- permissions
- freshness review
- conflicting evidence
- provenance
- editorial confidence

## Why this matters

When a factual passage changes, the author should be able to ask:

- What source supported this?
- Is the source still current?
- Is it primary or secondary?
- Is the wording a quotation?
- Does another source disagree?
- Does the claim need qualification?
