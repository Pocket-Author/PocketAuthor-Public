# Show the Transformation, Not the Hype

The strongest PocketAuthor marketing should demonstrate visible progress.

## Better proof formats

### Source → concept
Show notes becoming a clear book direction.

### Concept → outline
Show the reader, promise, thesis, and chapter spine.

### Outline → chapter
Show how sources and structure become draft material.

### Manuscript → publish-ready
Show editing, metadata, proofs, and edition files.

### Book → extended assets
Show the same approved ideas becoming a course, talk, newsletter, or launch campaign.

## Better than saying “AI-powered”

Show:

- what went in
- what decision was made
- what changed
- what the author approved
- what remains uncertain
- what the next milestone is
