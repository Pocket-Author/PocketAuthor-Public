# Keep the Thinking Connected From Source to Reader

A book project usually breaks apart across tools.

Notes in one place.
Research somewhere else.
Drafts in documents.
Comments in email.
Metadata in a spreadsheet.
Launch content in another workspace.

The PocketAuthor narrative is stronger when it focuses on continuity.

## One continuous workflow

### Gather
Collect permitted notes, documents, interviews, transcripts, and research.

### Shape
Find recurring ideas, reader needs, evidence gaps, and chapter structure.

### Create
Draft and revise with context nearby.

### Publish
Prepare metadata, files, and controlled editions.

### Extend
Create approved learning, marketing, and reader assets.

### Protect
Keep sources, rights, provenance, and human approval visible.

## Why continuity matters

Every handoff can lose context.

A connected system reduces the need to reconstruct why a decision was made.
