# Keep Human Approval Visible

AI can help with book work.

The important question is not whether AI appears in the workflow.

It is whether meaningful decisions remain reviewable and attributable.

## Approval should stay visible for

- book thesis
- major structural changes
- quotations
- factual claims
- source selection
- permissions
- sensitive stories
- title/subtitle
- metadata
- cover
- pricing
- distribution
- release

## Why this matters

Automation should reduce repetitive work without silently converting suggestions into publication decisions.
