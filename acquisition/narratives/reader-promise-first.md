# A Finished Book Starts With a Reader Promise

Before the outline, ask what the reader should receive.

## The promise should answer

- who the reader is
- what problem they have
- what the book helps them understand or do
- what makes the author credible
- what the book does not try to cover

## Why this matters

A weak promise creates:

- bloated outlines
- generic chapters
- unclear marketing
- confused titles
- mismatched readers
