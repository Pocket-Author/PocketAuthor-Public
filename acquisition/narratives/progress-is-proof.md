# Progress Is Better Proof Than Hype

For an authoring platform, visible progress is one of the strongest marketing assets.

## Proof can look like

- five notes becoming a book concept
- a concept becoming a structured outline
- an outline becoming a sourced chapter plan
- a draft becoming a reviewed manuscript
- a manuscript becoming print and ebook editions

## Why this works

The audience can see what changed.

It makes the product legible.

## Better product marketing question

Instead of:
“What feature should we announce?”

Ask:
“What transformation can we show?”
