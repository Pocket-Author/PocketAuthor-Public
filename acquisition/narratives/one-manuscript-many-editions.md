# One Manuscript, Many Editions

A paperback, EPUB, audiobook, translation, preview, and large-print edition should not become six disconnected books.

The stronger model is:

**one canonical manuscript → controlled editions**

## The canonical manuscript

The approved source of content.

Each edition can inherit from it while recording intentional differences.

## Edition-specific changes

A paperback may need:
- trim size
- margins
- gutter
- page breaks

An EPUB may need:
- responsive structure
- navigation
- accessible semantics

An audiobook may need:
- pronunciation guidance
- treatment of figures
- audio-specific wording

A translation may need:
- language review
- localization
- translator approval
- territory metadata

## Why this matters

When the master changes, the author should know which editions need review.
