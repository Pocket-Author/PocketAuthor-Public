# A Book Launch Is a System, Not a Day

Launch day is one point in a longer sequence.

## Before launch

- audience positioning
- waitlist
- landing page
- ARC program
- media list
- podcast outreach
- event planning
- partner outreach
- content calendar

## During launch

- reader communication
- events
- media
- partner amplification
- distribution checks
- customer support

## After launch

- workshops
- speaking
- newsletters
- long-tail search
- bulk sales
- course development
- edition fixes
- next title
