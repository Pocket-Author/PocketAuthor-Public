# Give Every Piece of Content One Job

A single post should not try to create awareness, explain the product, prove credibility, close the sale, and onboard the author.

## PocketAuthor funnel

### Discover
Is there a book in my work?

### Understand
What could my book become?

### Trust
Can this become professional?

### Build
Can I finish?

### Publish
Are my files ready?

### Expand
What happens after the book?

## Content rule

Every asset should have:

- one audience
- one pitch
- one funnel stage
- one CTA
- one proof requirement
- one success event

## Example

A “five notes → book outline” demo should not also explain ISBNs, grants, and book marketing.

Its job is to make the transformation believable.

**CTA:** Design content around one next action.
