# How to Write a Book Proposal

A book proposal explains why a book should exist, who it is for, why the author is positioned to write it, and how the project may fit a market.

## Common proposal elements

- overview
- reader/audience
- book promise
- author background
- comparable titles
- chapter outline
- sample chapters
- platform or reach
- market context
- manuscript status

## Important

Proposal expectations vary by genre, agent, and publisher.

Always check submission requirements before sending material.
