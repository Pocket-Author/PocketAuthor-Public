# How to Plan Bulk and Corporate Book Sales

Bulk sales can make sense for speakers, consultants, companies, associations, universities, and training programs.

## Potential buyers

- employers
- conferences
- associations
- schools
- universities
- nonprofits
- clients
- sponsors

## Prepare

- quantity tiers
- pricing
- shipping/fulfillment
- customization
- lead time
- invoices
- workshop bundles
- rights for special editions

## Sell the use case

A corporate buyer may care more about training, onboarding, event value, or client education than the book itself.
