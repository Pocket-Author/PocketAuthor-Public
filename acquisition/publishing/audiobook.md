# How to Prepare an Audiobook Edition

An audiobook is a new edition, not merely an audio export of the ebook.

## Decisions

- narrator
- author narration vs. professional narration
- pronunciation guide
- chapter structure
- footnotes/endnotes treatment
- tables/figures adaptation
- recording quality
- mastering
- metadata
- distribution
- rights

## Prepare the script

Mark:

- pronunciation
- difficult names
- acronyms
- visual-only passages
- citations that should be simplified for audio
