# How to Think About Translation Rights

Translation can extend a book into new languages and markets, but it creates new rights, editorial, and production decisions.

## Track

- language
- territory
- rights holder
- translator
- license terms
- approval rights
- title/cover changes
- local metadata
- royalties or fees
- edition status

## Translation is also editorial work

A strong translation may require localization, terminology review, cultural context, and author approval.
