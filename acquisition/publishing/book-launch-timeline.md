# How to Build a Book Launch Timeline

A book launch is easier when the work is sequenced before publication day.

## Early phase

- reader positioning
- cover/title direction
- landing page
- email capture
- partner list
- event opportunities
- ARC plan

## Pre-launch

- final metadata
- retailer/distributor setup
- ARC delivery
- media outreach
- podcast outreach
- event confirmations
- launch content

## Launch period

- email
- social
- events
- partner amplification
- reader support
- distribution checks

## Post-launch

- continued content
- speaking
- bulk sales
- workshops
- reviews
- edition fixes
- long-tail discovery
