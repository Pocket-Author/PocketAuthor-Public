# What Is an ARC?

An ARC—advance reader copy—is a pre-publication version of a book shared with selected readers before release.

## Possible uses

- reviews
- blurbs
- media outreach
- bookseller outreach
- early reader feedback
- launch preparation

## Track

- recipient
- delivery date
- edition/version
- follow-up date
- response
- permission to quote
- corrections discovered

## Important

An ARC is not necessarily the final edition.

Make that clear to recipients where relevant.
