# How to Handle Copyright Permissions in a Book

Books can include material created by other people: quotations, images, tables, lyrics, illustrations, interviews, letters, and more.

## Review

- who created the material
- who owns the rights
- what you want to use
- how much you want to use
- where the book will be distributed
- whether permission is required
- whether attribution is required
- whether a license has conditions

## Do not guess

Copyright exceptions are fact- and jurisdiction-specific.

Seek qualified legal advice when the use is important or unclear.
