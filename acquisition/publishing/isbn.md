# What Is an ISBN and When Do You Need One?

An ISBN is an identifier for a specific edition and format of a book.

Authors often encounter ISBN decisions when preparing print and ebook editions.

## What to decide

- which formats you are publishing
- whether each format needs its own identifier
- who will be listed as the publisher or imprint
- whether to use an identifier supplied by a platform or obtain your own
- which territories and distribution channels matter

## Important

ISBN rules and options can vary by market and distribution route. Always confirm current requirements with the relevant ISBN agency, retailer, or distributor.
