# Responsible Authoring

PocketAuthor is being designed to help people use AI while maintaining clear human responsibility for authorship.

## Source tracking

Authors should be able to distinguish:

- original author knowledge
- interviews
- primary documents
- research papers
- books
- web sources
- AI-generated suggestions
- unattributed notes requiring review

## Quotations

Direct quotations should be identifiable, attributable, and reviewed for appropriate use and permissions.

## Paraphrasing

Paraphrasing should express ideas in genuinely original language rather than merely disguise copied wording.

## Attribution

Where a concept, fact, framework, or quotation comes from another source, authors should be able to trace where it came from and determine whether attribution is appropriate or required.

## Fact checking

AI-generated text can be wrong. Serious authoring workflows should flag claims that require verification and make it easier to review evidence.

## Copyright

Copyright treatment depends on jurisdiction and context. PocketAuthor can help surface potential risks and organize remediation, but it should not present automated output as a substitute for qualified legal advice.

## Better alternatives to weak sourcing

Where possible, authors should strengthen books by moving upstream:

- replace summaries with primary sources
- interview original experts
- request permission for important material
- cite authoritative records
- recreate ideas through original analysis
- remove unsupported claims
- distinguish fact from opinion

## Human approval

The author should remain the final editorial authority. AI-assisted text should be reviewed before publication.

## Privacy

Never place confidential manuscripts, private source material, credentials, protected personal information, or sensitive interviews into public issue trackers.
