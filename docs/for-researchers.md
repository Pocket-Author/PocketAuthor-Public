# PocketAuthor for Researchers

Research should not disappear inside disconnected PDFs, conference talks, or institutional archives.

PocketAuthor is designed to help researchers turn rigorous work into accessible, source-grounded books.

## Research-oriented needs

- literature organization
- primary and secondary source separation
- citation tracking
- source provenance
- interview integration
- dataset notes
- uncertainty labeling
- fact checking
- glossary management
- claim review
- accessible explanation

## Research paper ≠ public book

A book needs more than additional length. It needs:

- a reader journey
- contextual explanation
- narrative progression
- examples
- consequences
- human stories
- explicit limitations
- a reason the research matters outside the field

PocketAuthor helps organize that translation without hiding where the evidence came from.
