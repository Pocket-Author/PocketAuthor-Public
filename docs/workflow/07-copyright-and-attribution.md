# 7. Copyright and Attribution

Books combine original writing with ideas, quotations, interviews, images, frameworks, and research created by others.

## Review questions

- Is this wording original?
- Is this a direct quotation?
- Is attribution needed?
- Is permission needed?
- Is the source traceable?
- Is the paraphrase genuinely original?
- Is this framework someone else's protected or branded work?
- Can a primary source replace a secondary summary?
- Does public-domain status vary by jurisdiction?
- Is there a better original interview or source?

PocketAuthor can support review and organization, but automated guidance is not legal advice.
