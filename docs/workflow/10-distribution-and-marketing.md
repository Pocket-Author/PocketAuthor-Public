# 10. Distribution and Marketing

A finished book still needs a path to readers.

## Distribution options

- Amazon KDP
- IngramSpark
- direct website/store sales
- bulk and corporate sales
- course/community bundles
- audiobook channels
- international and translation editions

## Launch foundation

- reader and market positioning
- subtitle and description
- author bio
- metadata
- landing page
- email capture
- launch calendar
- advance-reader plan

## Turn the book into a content engine

Each chapter can produce:

- essays
- newsletters
- podcast pitches
- social scripts
- carousels
- quote graphics
- webinars
- workshops
- course lessons
- speaking abstracts

A launch should begin before publication, not after it.
