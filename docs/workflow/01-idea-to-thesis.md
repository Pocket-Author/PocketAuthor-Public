# 1. Idea to Thesis

A topic is not yet a book.

A strong book begins with a thesis: what the author believes, teaches, reveals, documents, or helps the reader accomplish.

## Ask

- Who is the reader?
- What do they struggle with?
- What should change after reading?
- What does the author know from direct experience?
- What needs outside evidence?
- Why does this book need to exist now?
- What is explicitly outside scope?

## Deliverable: the Book Brief

Capture:

- working title
- audience
- reader problem
- book promise
- central thesis
- unique perspective
- evidence base
- tone
- exclusions
- desired outcome
