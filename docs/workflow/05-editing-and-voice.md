# 5. Editing and Voice

Editing should make a book more clearly the author's—not more generically "AI polished."

## Editing layers

1. **Developmental editing** — structure, argument, reader journey.
2. **Chapter editing** — coherence, pacing, repetition.
3. **Line editing** — clarity, rhythm, style.
4. **Copy editing** — grammar, terminology, consistency.
5. **Proofreading** — final publication errors.

## Voice check

Review voice across the whole manuscript:

- Does the author sound recognizably like themselves?
- Are signature phrases overused?
- Has nuance been flattened?
- Is vocabulary consistent with the author's field?
- Are stories still told from the author's perspective?
- Did revisions accidentally make every chapter sound the same?
