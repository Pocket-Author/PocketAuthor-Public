# 4. Drafting

Drafting turns architecture and evidence into prose.

## Good chapter inputs

- chapter objective
- thesis
- author's notes
- stories
- quotations
- research
- source references
- examples
- desired tone
- transition from previous chapter

## Drafting principle

Do not optimize the first pass for perfection.

Optimize for:

- completeness
- coherence
- traceability
- author voice
- clear gaps for later review

AI assistance can accelerate expansion and synthesis, but the author remains responsible for what the book says.
