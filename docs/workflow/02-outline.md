# 2. Build the Outline

The outline is the architecture of the reader's transformation.

## Every chapter should answer

- Why does this chapter exist?
- What question does the reader have here?
- What is the central claim?
- What evidence supports it?
- What story or example makes it memorable?
- What should the reader understand or do afterward?
- Why does the next chapter follow?

## A useful test

If two chapters can be swapped without changing the reader journey, the architecture may still be weak.

The outline is also where you identify missing research before drafting hundreds of pages.
