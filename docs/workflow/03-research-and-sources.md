# 3. Research and Sources

Research should strengthen original authorship—not bury it.

## Source types

- primary documents
- interviews
- datasets
- academic papers
- books
- official records
- industry reports
- journalism
- archival material
- public web sources
- personal notes
- field observations

## Track provenance

For important claims, preserve:

- source
- author
- publication date
- page or section
- URL or identifier
- access date where useful
- notes
- permission status
- confidence

## Separate clearly

- verified fact
- interpretation
- author recollection
- hypothesis
- opinion
- disputed claim
- AI suggestion requiring verification
