# PocketAuthor Objection Handling

## “I can just use ChatGPT.”

You can use a general AI assistant for many individual writing tasks.

A book project also needs structure, sources, revisions, interviews, approvals, production, metadata, rights, distribution and continuity across months of work.

PocketAuthor is built around the book as the organizing object.

---

## “I do not want AI writing my book for me.”

That is a reasonable concern.

PocketAuthor's strongest positioning is not “let AI write your book.”

It is:

**use AI to reduce the coordination and repetitive work around authorship while keeping the author responsible for the ideas, voice, facts and final decisions.**

---

## “I already have an editor.”

Good.

PocketAuthor should make the editor's work easier by organizing drafts, sources, revision stages, comments and approvals.

It is not positioned as a substitute for professional editing.

---

## “I already have a ghostwriter.”

PocketAuthor can act as shared project infrastructure for interviews, source material, voice references, claim review, approvals and production.

The ghostwriter remains the professional doing the work.

---

## “I only need a place to write.”

If a simple text editor is enough, use one.

PocketAuthor is most useful when the manuscript is connected to:
- source material
- research
- revisions
- collaborators
- editions
- publishing
- marketing
- rights
- opportunities

## “How do I find a publisher?”

Use:
https://www.pocketauthor.com/publisher-directory

---

## “Will PocketAuthor get my book into bookstores?”

No platform should promise that.

PocketAuthor can help authors understand production, distribution, publishers, stores and submission workflows.

Actual stocking, retailer acceptance and sell-through depend on external decisions.

---

## “Who owns my manuscript?”

PocketAuthor's public copyright page states that original manuscript content submitted by authors remains theirs, subject to the service terms and limited processing rights needed to operate the service.

See:
https://www.pocketauthor.com/copyright

---

## “Will PocketAuthor guarantee sales?”

No.

The platform can help with planning, positioning, launch systems, events, distribution and derivative assets.

It should not guarantee sales, bestseller status, media coverage or retailer placement.
