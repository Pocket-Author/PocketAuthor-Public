## Friendly hero copy

You have probably already done more of the work than you think.

PocketAuthor helps you gather what you already know, find the book inside it, build the manuscript, check the sources, prepare real editions and understand what happens after the writing is finished.

## Founder hero

Your company history is full of decisions, failures, lessons and frameworks.

PocketAuthor helps you find the book inside that archive—and turn it into something useful beyond the company timeline.

## Consultant hero

Your best framework should not live only inside client calls.

Turn the ideas people already hire you for into a book, workshop, course and durable body of work.

## Creator hero

Your audience sees fragments.

Your book can show the whole argument.

Turn podcasts, newsletters, videos and years of content into something coherent enough to keep.

## Researcher hero

Make the research easier to read without making it less rigorous.

Keep sources, evidence, uncertainty and provenance connected to the manuscript.

## Educator hero

Turn the course you have taught for years into a book learners can keep.

## Ghostwriter hero

Keep interviews, voice references, claims and approvals connected to the manuscript so the engagement does not become spreadsheet archaeology.

## Editor hero

Know which stage of revision you are in, what changed, what the author approved and what still needs attention.

## Publisher hero

One approved manuscript. Multiple controlled editions. Clear handoffs from editorial to production.

## Bookstore hero

Books do not end at distribution platforms.

Help readers discover the stores, clubs and events where literary communities actually live.

## Literary organization hero

Make the ecosystem around books easier to discover: presses, festivals, libraries, workshops, events and communities.

## Short brand descriptions

### 15 words
PocketAuthor helps people turn existing expertise into books and navigate the work around publishing them.

### 30 words
PocketAuthor is an authoring and publishing workspace that connects source material, writing, research, editing, production, publishers, grants, events, rights and book-growth workflows around one project.

### 60 words
PocketAuthor helps founders, experts, creators, researchers and professional book teams turn existing knowledge into finished books. The platform connects source material, outlining, writing, research, editing, provenance, production, publisher discovery, grants, literary events, rights, distribution and derivative assets so the author can understand and control the full journey around the manuscript.
