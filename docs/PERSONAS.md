# PocketAuthor Personas

PocketAuthor serves different kinds of authors and book professionals. The product story should change depending on what each person is trying to accomplish.

## Founders

### What they already have
- pitch decks
- product memos
- investor updates
- talks
- strategy documents
- customer stories
- operating frameworks
- market lessons

### What they want
A book that turns hard-earned experience into authority, category definition, recruiting leverage, speaking opportunities, and durable company knowledge.

### Best message
**A founder's archive is often an unwritten book.**

The company story itself may not be the book. The framework behind it might be.
