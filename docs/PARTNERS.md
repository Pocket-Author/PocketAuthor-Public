# PocketAuthor for Editors, Ghostwriters, Publishers, and Professional Partners

PocketAuthor can also serve the professionals who help other people make books.

## The partner problem

Editors, ghostwriters, researchers, and publishing teams often become the human integration layer across:

- manuscript files
- interviews
- research
- notes
- source tracking
- revisions
- client approvals
- designers
- proofing
- metadata
- publishing
- launch assets

## Infrastructure opportunity

PocketAuthor can help professional partners create repeatable delivery systems:

- reusable project templates
- source libraries
- milestone workflows
- author approvals
- editorial handoffs
- rights and provenance review
- production checklists
- publication readiness
- derivative content
- repeat-title workflows

## Potential partner models

- editor workspaces
- ghostwriter workspaces
- small-publisher cohorts
- referral programs
- professional marketplace
- managed production
- agency workflows
- reusable client templates

## Positioning

PocketAuthor should not claim to replace professional editors, ghostwriters, designers, or publishers.

It should help them deliver better work with less coordination overhead.
