## LinkedIn — Founder Archive

A founder's archive is often an unwritten book.

But the company story is not automatically the book.

The useful book may be:

- the framework you developed
- the market truth you learned the hard way
- the technical system nobody has explained well
- the mistakes the next generation should avoid
- the category thesis your industry still misunderstands

The work is finding the durable idea inside the chronology.

That is a much better starting point than: "Ask AI to write my startup story."

## LinkedIn — AI Authorship

AI can help write a book.

That does not mean AI should become the author.

The strongest AI-assisted publishing workflow should make it easier to see:

- where a claim came from
- what needs verification
- what is a quotation
- what requires permission
- what came from an interview
- what reflects the author's direct experience
- what the author explicitly approved

Speed is useful.

Accountability is part of quality.
