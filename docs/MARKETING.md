# PocketAuthor Marketing System

## Core story

**From body of work to book.**

PocketAuthor marketing should begin with the transformation authors want:

**Turn years of scattered expertise into a finished, credible book—and turn that book into a reusable library of intellectual property.**

The product should not be marketed as generic AI output.

It should be marketed around:

- authorship
- completion
- ownership
- evidence
- source provenance
- editorial control
- production readiness
- distribution
- book-to-business leverage

## Core hooks

- You may already have a book hiding in your notes, talks, and client work.
- A founder's archive is often an unwritten book.
- The company story is not the book. The hard-earned framework might be.
- Your book should not start from a blank page. It should start from your body of work.
- AI can accelerate book production. It cannot replace authorship, judgment, or provenance.
- The manuscript is only one part of the author workload.
- Authors should own the creative decisions without becoming the human integration layer for every publishing task.
- A finished book is not the end. It can become a launch system, teaching asset, course, workshop, speaking platform, and next title.

## Transformation ladder

1. **Discover** — Is there a book in my work?
2. **Understand** — What would my book be?
3. **Trust** — Can this become professional?
4. **Build** — Can I finish it?
5. **Publish** — Are my files ready?
6. **Expand** — What can the book become after publication?

## Content pillars

- book ideas hidden inside existing work
- source material → concept → outline → chapter
- before-and-after transformations
- author and founder journeys
- practical publishing education
- responsible AI and provenance
- publishing readiness
- distribution
- rights and editions
- book-to-business leverage

## Weekly publishing rhythm

- **Monday — Discovery:** provocative recognition of the book hiding in existing work.
- **Tuesday — Education:** checklist, answer, framework, or tutorial.
- **Wednesday — Proof:** workflow demonstration or consented author transformation.
- **Thursday — Conversion:** workshop, Book Potential Assessment, Blueprint, or manuscript-start CTA.
- **Friday — Trust:** sourced guide, responsible-AI lesson, publishing myth, or production-readiness education.

## Rule for every piece of marketing

Every asset should contain:

- one audience
- one pitch
- one funnel stage
- one CTA
- one proof requirement
- one success event

Do not optimize content primarily for impressions. Optimize for authors moving toward finished books.
