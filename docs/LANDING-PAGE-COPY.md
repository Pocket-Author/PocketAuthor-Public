## Transformation

### Go from scattered source material to a publishable system.

**Discover** the strongest book concept.

**Blueprint** the reader, promise, thesis, and chapter architecture.

**Research** with sources attached to claims.

**Draft** without losing your voice.

**Edit** across the full manuscript.

**Prepare** professional publishing files and metadata.

**Distribute** through the channels that fit the book.

**Expand** the book into talks, workshops, courses, content, and future titles.

- revision

- distribution
- launch strategy

AI should make that system easier to manage—not make authorship meaningless.
