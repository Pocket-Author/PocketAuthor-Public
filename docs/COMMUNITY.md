# PocketAuthor Community

PocketAuthor's public community direction includes educational and collaborative formats that help more people become authors.

## Community formats

- live demos
- writing sessions
- founder-author cohorts
- workshops
- office hours
- publishing clinics
- research sessions
- conference activations
- collaborative public books
- partner events

## Writers Blocks

A writing session format can combine focused writing time, peer feedback, and practical publishing progress.

## Demo sessions

Public demos should show real author workflows: idea development, outlining, research, drafting, editing, source review, and publishing preparation.

## Cohorts

Cohorts can help authors move through milestones together with accountability and structured guidance.

## Conference activations

PocketAuthor can be used at conferences and community events to help participants:

- capture a book idea
- build an outline
- write a page
- contribute to collaborative books
- document domain knowledge
- leave with a concrete next step

## Partnerships

Potential partners include:

- universities
- accelerators
- founder communities
- professional associations
- nonprofits
- libraries
- writing organizations
- research communities
- publishers
- conferences
