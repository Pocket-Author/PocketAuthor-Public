# PocketAuthor Campaign Library

## 1. The Book Hiding in Your Work

**Hook:** You may already have a book hiding in your notes, talks, and client work.

**Visual:** five source artifacts → three themes → one book promise → chapter outline.

**CTA:** Take the Book Potential Assessment.

---

## 2. Source Material to Book Blueprint

**Hook:** A blank page is the wrong place to start when you already have years of work.

**Flow:** notes → recurring ideas → reader → promise → chapter spine → research gaps.

**CTA:** Create a Book Blueprint.

---

## 3. The Founder Archive

**Hook:** A founder's archive is often an unwritten book.

**Flow:** approved decks, notes, talks, product decisions, research, and hard-earned lessons → framework → manuscript.

**CTA:** Find the book inside your company history.

---

## 4. Beyond the Manuscript

**Hook:** The manuscript is only one part of the author workload.

Show:
- research
- sources
- revisions
- permissions
- rights
- metadata
- proofing
- production
- distribution
- launch
- derivative content

**CTA:** Read the Publishing Readiness Guide.

---

## 5. AI Without Losing Authorship

**Hook:** AI can accelerate book production. It cannot replace judgment, provenance, lived experience, or responsibility.

**CTA:** Read the Responsible Authoring Guide.

---

## 6. Book to Business

**Hook:** Your book can become a workshop, keynote, course, lead engine, community, training system, and next title.

**CTA:** Map your book-to-business system.

---

## 7. Can Your Idea Sustain a Book?

Ask:
- Is there a specific reader?
- Is there a meaningful transformation?
- Do you have original experience?
- Is there enough evidence?
- Are there distinct chapters?
- What does the reader know or do afterward?

**CTA:** Take the Book Potential Assessment.

---

## 8. Five Notes to Three Book Ideas

**Hook:** Give us five pieces of your existing work. What books might already be hiding inside them?

Possible inputs:
- article
- deck
- transcript
- memo
- podcast
- research note

**CTA:** Start with your existing work.

---

## 9. Publish-Ready Is Different From Finished

**Hook:** "The draft is done" and "the book is ready to publish" are not the same milestone.

Show the gap between manuscript completion and:
- permissions
- proofing
- formatting
- metadata
- cover
- ISBN
- print files
- ebook files
- distribution setup

**CTA:** Run the readiness checklist.
