# PocketAuthor

**From body of work to book.**

PocketAuthor is building the infrastructure that helps founders, experts, creators, researchers, educators, and professionals turn years of knowledge into finished books.

We focus on the full author journey:

**source material → Book Blueprint → outline → manuscript → editing → provenance → publishing → distribution → book-to-business**

## Why PocketAuthor

The future of authorship is not one-click AI books.

It is better infrastructure for human authors.

PocketAuthor is designed around:

- author voice
- long-form continuity
- research and sources
- interviews and notes
- editorial workflows
- fact checking
- copyright and provenance
- publishing readiness
- distribution
- marketing
- rights
- derivative intellectual property

## Our belief

A book should preserve what is uniquely human about its author: lived experience, judgment, reporting, research, voice, conviction, and responsibility.

AI should make the surrounding work easier—not make authorship less meaningful.
