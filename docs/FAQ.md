# PocketAuthor FAQ

## What is PocketAuthor?
PocketAuthor is an AI-assisted authoring platform focused on helping people turn expertise, notes, interviews, research, and ideas into publishable books.

## Can AI really help write a book?
Yes, especially with structure, synthesis, editing, brainstorming, gap detection, and repetitive editorial work. High-quality books still require human judgment, experience, reporting, research, verification, and revision.

## Does PocketAuthor write the whole book for me?
PocketAuthor is designed as an authoring system, not a one-click replacement for authors. Different authors may use different levels of assistance.

## Can I preserve my own voice?
Voice preservation is a major product goal. The platform should help authors provide examples, style guidance, preferences, and project context so assistance remains aligned with the author.

## Can I work from transcripts or interviews?
That is a key use case. Interviews can contain unique primary-source material that strengthens a book significantly.

## Can PocketAuthor help with research?
Research organization, source tracking, evidence review, and fact checking are major product directions.

## How does PocketAuthor approach copyright?
The goal is to help authors identify source dependencies, quotation risks, attribution needs, provenance questions, and possible remediation steps. It is not a replacement for legal counsel.

## Is PocketAuthor only for nonfiction?
Nonfiction is a major focus because of research, source, and expertise workflows, but many authoring concepts also apply to other categories.

## What is the difference between PocketAuthor and a chatbot?
A chatbot operates around conversations. PocketAuthor is being designed around persistent book projects, chapter structure, editorial workflows, research, revisions, publishing, and distribution.

## Can teams collaborate?
Collaboration among authors, editors, researchers, and other contributors is part of the broader product direction.

## How long does it take to write a book?
There is no universal answer. A short expertise-driven book with existing source material may move quickly; deeply researched books can take much longer. PocketAuthor aims to compress avoidable workflow time without pretending quality has no cost.

## Does PocketAuthor publish the book?
Publishing preparation and connected workflows are part of the product direction. Exact publishing and distribution capabilities may evolve.

## Does PocketAuthor help with book marketing?
Author marketing, launch preparation, positioning, metadata, and distribution are part of the larger author workflow.

## Is my manuscript private?
Users should review the current PocketAuthor privacy policy and product terms before placing sensitive material into any software system.

## Who owns my book?
Authors should retain clear control over their work, subject to the current product terms. Always review current terms before publishing.

## Can I export my work?
Portable manuscript export is an important author requirement and part of the intended product philosophy.
