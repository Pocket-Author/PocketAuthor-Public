# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Founders

## Core message
**A founder's archive is often an unwritten book.**

The company story itself may not be the book.

The durable framework behind the company might be.

## Pain hooks
- Your best thinking is trapped in decks, memos, and repeated conversations.
- You keep explaining the same market lesson from scratch.
- The company history contains insight that may disappear when the team changes.
- Your category thesis lives in fragments.
- You have years of founder lessons but no durable place they all belong.

## Outcome hooks
- Turn company-building lessons into a book that outlives the startup timeline.
- Package category insight into a durable point of view.
- Turn founder experience into speaking, recruiting, customer education, and thought leadership.
- Make your operating philosophy inspectable.
- Preserve what the company taught you before the context disappears.

## Proof angles
- show five founder artifacts becoming one book thesis
- show pitch-deck language becoming reader-facing chapter logic
- show company chronology becoming a reusable framework
- show what gets removed because it is confidential or irrelevant

## Hero
**Turn company-building experience into a book worth keeping.**

PocketAuthor helps founders turn product decisions, customer lessons, market research, talks, memos, failures, and hard-earned judgment into a structured book—with the research, publishing, and next steps connected.

**CTA:** Find the book inside your founder archive.

## LinkedIn post
