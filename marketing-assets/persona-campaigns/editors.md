# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Editors

## Core message
**Different editorial stages need different finish lines.**

## Pain hooks
- Authors ask for “editing” without defining the stage.
- Source context gets lost.
- Revision history becomes impossible to reconstruct.
- Comments become endless discussion.
- Production receives a manuscript with unresolved editorial issues.

## Outcome hooks
- Separate developmental, line, copy, fact-check, and proof stages.
- Keep chapter status and author approval visible.
- Connect evidence to passages.
- Make editorial handoffs clearer.
- Preserve editorial memory across revisions.

## Hero
**Edit the book with the whole project context visible.**

PocketAuthor helps connect manuscript stages, source context, comments, revisions, author decisions, and production handoff around one book.
