# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Publishers and Small Presses

## Core message
**One approved manuscript. Multiple controlled editions.**

## Pain hooks
- Editorial and production live in disconnected systems.
- Metadata drifts between teams.
- Edition changes are difficult to synchronize.
- Author approvals become hard to trace.
- Rights and production status live in separate spreadsheets.

## Outcome hooks
- Keep one canonical title project.
- Connect editorial, metadata, rights, production, and editions.
- Reduce version drift.
- Make author approvals explicit.
- Build repeatable title workflows for small presses.

## Hero
**Move from approved manuscript to controlled editions without losing the source of truth.**

PocketAuthor is exploring book-native infrastructure for editorial, metadata, rights, production, and author-facing workflows.
