# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Creators

## Core message
**Your feed is fragmented by design. A book rewards coherence.**

## Pain hooks
- Years of content disappear into old feeds.
- Your best ideas are spread across formats.
- You repeat the same thesis without ever assembling it.
- The algorithm rewards fragments, not depth.
- Your archive is large but difficult to reuse.

## Outcome hooks
- Turn years of content into one durable argument.
- Make your best ideas easier to find and keep.
- Turn a podcast, newsletter, or channel into source material.
- Build the book that explains the whole idea.
- Let the book become the canonical source for future content.
