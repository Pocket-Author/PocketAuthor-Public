# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Ghostwriters

## Core message
**Ghostwriting is an evidence and trust practice.**

## Pain hooks
- Interviews, notes, claims, approvals, and revisions live everywhere.
- Voice references are inconsistent.
- Client memory needs verification.
- Approval cycles become difficult to track.
- The ghostwriter becomes the human integration layer.

## Outcome hooks
- Keep interviews, voice, claims, and approvals connected to the manuscript.
- Spend less time coordinating the engagement.
- Make client approval visible.
- Preserve source trails and project history.
- Build repeatable professional delivery workflows.

## Hero
**Manage the engagement around the manuscript—not just the manuscript.**

PocketAuthor's ghostwriting workflows can help organize interviews, transcripts, voice references, evidence, approvals, revisions, and milestones around one book project.
