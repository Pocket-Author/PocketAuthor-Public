# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Researchers

## Core message
**Make rigorous research readable without making it less rigorous.**

## Pain hooks
- Your work is trapped behind disciplinary language.
- Years of research are spread across papers and notes.
- Public audiences need context your papers were never designed to provide.
- You want accessibility without sacrificing evidence.
- Citations, interviews, and claims become difficult to manage at book scale.

## Outcome hooks
- Turn a body of research into one coherent public argument.
- Keep evidence and provenance connected to the manuscript.
- Translate complexity without flattening uncertainty.
- Create a book that makes serious research usable outside the field.
- Preserve source discipline while changing the reading experience.

## Hero
**Turn years of research into a book more people can understand.**

Organize papers, notes, interviews, datasets, literature, and source trails into a reader-facing manuscript without abandoning rigor.

**CTA:** Build the public argument inside your research.

## LinkedIn post
A research paper and a public-facing book have different jobs.

A paper proves something inside a discipline.

A book may need to explain:

- why the question matters
- what the reader needs to know first
- how the evidence connects
- where uncertainty remains
- what changes because of the findings

Accessibility should not require intellectual dishonesty.
