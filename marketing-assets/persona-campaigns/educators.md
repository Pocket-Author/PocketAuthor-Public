# PocketAuthor Campaign Pack — Educators

## Core message
**Turn years of teaching into a book learners can keep.**

## Pain hooks
- Great teaching disappears when the semester ends.
- Lessons live across slides, LMS pages, and worksheets.
- Students need a durable reference.
- Course content has no coherent long-form version.
- You keep rebuilding the same explanations.

## Outcome hooks
- Turn curriculum into a book or workbook.
- Convert lectures into durable learning resources.
- Build exercises and review questions into the book.
- Turn the book into a course companion.
- Preserve teaching knowledge across cohorts.

## Hero
**Turn the course you have taught for years into a book.**

PocketAuthor helps educators organize learning objectives, lessons, examples, exercises, references, and companion resources into a durable teaching artifact.

**CTA:** Build the book version of your course.

## Social post
A course disappears by semester.

A book can survive the cohort.

Your lectures, exercises, examples, and student questions may already contain a resource worth preserving.
