# PocketAuthor Event Follow-Up Sequences

## After “Find the Book Inside Your Body of Work”

### Message 1 — same day
Thanks for joining.

The most important thing to keep from the session is this:

You do not need to start from an empty page.

Start with the material you already have, identify the reader, and decide what the book should help that reader understand or do.

Your next step:
write the one-sentence promise of the book.

### Message 2 — 2 days later
One question that often appears after the workshop:

“How do I know whether this idea is big enough for a book?”

A useful test:

- clear reader
- real problem
- distinctive experience
- evidence
- multiple non-repetitive chapters
- a meaningful change for the reader

Do not measure the idea by how many words you can generate.

Measure it by whether the reader journey has enough substance.

### CTA
Build the outline.

---

### Message 3 — 5 days later
Your source material does not need to be organized before you begin.

Bring:

- notes
- transcripts
- talks
- research
- frameworks
- drafts

Then let the structure emerge from the reader promise.

## After Manuscript-to-Publish Workshop

### Message 1
A finished manuscript is a major milestone.

The next phase is production.

Your checklist should now include:
editing, permissions, metadata, identifiers, cover, ebook, print, proof, and distribution.

### CTA
https://www.pocketauthor.com/how-to-publish

---

### Message 2
Do not solve every publishing decision at once.

Choose the next unresolved stage.

Then complete it before moving downstream.

Publishing becomes much easier when the chain is visible.
