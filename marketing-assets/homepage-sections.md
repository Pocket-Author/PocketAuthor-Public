# PocketAuthor Homepage Section Copy

## Section — The problem

### Your knowledge is already everywhere.

It lives in notes, decks, transcripts, research folders, interviews, documents, and years of experience.

The hard part is not always generating more words.

The hard part is turning all of that into one coherent book.

---

## Section — The workflow

### One connected path from source to reader.

**Gather**  
Bring in the work you already have.

**Shape**  
Find the reader, promise, thesis, and structure.

**Create**  
Draft with sources and context nearby.

**Review**  
Edit, verify, and preserve provenance.

**Publish**  
Prepare metadata, files, proofs, and editions.

**Extend**  
Turn the book into workshops, courses, talks, and future titles.

---

## Section — Why not generic AI?

### A book is more than generated text.

A serious book needs continuity, evidence, revisions, permissions, collaboration, metadata, production, and distribution.

PocketAuthor is built around the project—not the isolated prompt.

---

## Section — Responsible AI

### AI should strengthen authorship, not obscure it.

Use automation for organization, synthesis, drafting, and repetitive work.

Keep human approval visible for the decisions that matter.

---
