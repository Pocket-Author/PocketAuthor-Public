# PocketAuthor Workshop and Event Copy

## Workshop — Find the Book Inside Your Body of Work

### Title
Find the Book Inside Your Body of Work

### Promise
Bring your notes, talks, research, presentations, frameworks, interviews, or existing content. Leave with a clearer book direction, reader promise, and initial chapter spine.

### Who it is for
- founders
- consultants
- researchers
- educators
- creators
- professionals with years of accumulated expertise

### Agenda
1. Source inventory
2. Recurring problems
3. Reader selection
4. Book promise
5. Book type
6. Chapter spine
7. Evidence gaps
8. Next writing milestone

### CTA
Bring five pieces of work you think might belong in a book.

## Workshop — Is There a Book Here?

### Hook
Before you commit months to writing, test whether the idea can sustain a useful book.

### Participants leave with
- target reader
- core promise
- author advantage
- source inventory
- potential book type
- initial chapter structure
- risk/gap list

### CTA
Take the Book Potential Assessment before the session.

## Workshop — Book to Business

### Promise
Map how a finished book can become a workshop, course, speaking platform, client education system, bulk-sales offer, or next title.

### Exercise
Build a one-page IP map from the book’s strongest frameworks.

### CTA
