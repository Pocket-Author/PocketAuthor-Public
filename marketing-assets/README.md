# PocketAuthor Marketing Asset Library

This folder contains **22 reusable marketing asset files** plus this index, built from the PocketAuthor positioning, acquisition library, live product surfaces, blog themes, and content calendar.

## Core funnel

### Discover
Help someone recognize that a book may already exist inside their work.

### Understand
Show what that book could become.

### Trust
Demonstrate workflow, sources, publishing readiness, and responsible AI.

### Build
Move the author into a real manuscript workflow.

### Publish
Help the author prepare editions, metadata, proofing, and distribution.

### Expand
Turn the finished book into a durable professional asset.

## Claims guardrails

Do not use:
- guaranteed publication
- guaranteed retailer acceptance
- guaranteed sales
- fabricated bestseller claims
- invented testimonials
- unsupported author outcomes

Use consent and context for case studies and author stories.
