# PocketAuthor Marketing Asset Library

This folder contains reusable forward-facing marketing assets built from the PocketAuthor positioning, acquisition library, blog themes, and content calendar.

## Asset families

- Persona campaigns
- Email nurture
- Social posts
- Reels / TikTok / Shorts
- Carousel copy
- Workshops and events
- Lead magnets
- Partner outreach
- Launch sequences
- Conversion copy
- Founder-led marketing

## Core funnel

### Discover
Help someone recognize that a book may already exist inside their work.

### Understand
Show what that book could become.

### Trust
Demonstrate workflow, sources, publishing readiness, and responsible AI.

### Build
Move the author into a real manuscript workflow.

### Publish
Help the author prepare editions, metadata, proofing, and distribution.

### Expand
Turn the finished book into a durable professional asset.
