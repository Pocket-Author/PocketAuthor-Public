# PocketAuthor Partner Outreach Copy

## Editors

### Subject
A book workspace built for the work around editing

PocketAuthor is building book-native infrastructure for authors and professional editors.

The goal is not to replace editorial judgment.

It is to reduce the coordination burden around:

- manuscript versions
- source context
- comments
- review stages
- author approval
- production handoff

We are looking for editors who want to help shape workflows that actually match professional book work.

Explore the public role guide:
https://www.pocketauthor.com/book-professionals

## Publishers / Small Presses

### Subject
A controlled workflow from approved manuscript to multiple editions

PocketAuthor is exploring book-native production infrastructure for small presses and publishing teams.

Core idea:

**one approved manuscript → controlled print, ebook, audio, and translation workflows**

We are especially interested in teams dealing with production bottlenecks across editorial, metadata, rights, and editions.

Publisher ecosystem:
