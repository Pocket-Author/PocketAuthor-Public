# PocketAuthor Case Study Template

## Title
How [Author / Persona] Turned [Source Material] Into [Book Outcome]

## 1. Starting point

Describe:
- author role
- existing source material
- book goal
- constraints
- project stage

Avoid invented metrics or exaggerated before/after language.

## 2. The problem

What was preventing progress?

Examples:
- too much scattered material
- unclear book thesis
- outline not coherent
- research disconnected
- source tracking weak
- manuscript complete but not publish-ready

## 3. The source material

Show the real inputs, with consent:

- notes
- talks
- interviews
- research
- decks
- drafts

## 4. The workflow

Document the actual steps:

source inventory  
→ book direction  
→ Blueprint  
→ outline  
→ chapter plan  
→ draft  
→ review  
→ publication readiness

## 5. What changed

Use observable changes:

- clearer reader
- narrower scope
- approved outline
- missing research identified
- first chapter completed
- production checklist completed

Avoid unsupported claims like:
- guaranteed faster
- bestseller
- increased revenue without evidence

## 6. Author quote

Only use a real, approved quote.

Include context.

## 7. Artifacts

Show, when approved:
- before source
- after outline
- chapter map
- publishing checklist
- final book

## 8. CTA

Invite the reader to take the same next step:
- Book Potential Assessment
- Book Blueprint
- workshop
- source-material workflow
