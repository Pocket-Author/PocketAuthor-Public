# PocketAuthor Campaign Measurement Brief

## Do not optimize only for reach

Reach is context.

The stronger question is whether content moves the right author toward a real book milestone.

## Funnel measures

### Discover
- qualified reach
- profile visits
- article reads
- diagnostic starts

### Understand
- Book Opportunity completion
- Book Blueprint starts
- workflow-demo engagement
- guide depth

### Trust
- newsletter subscription
- workshop registration
- return visits
- sourced-guide engagement

### Build
- account creation
- active manuscript
- outline approval
- first chapter completion

### Publish
- publishing-readiness completion
- proof/export completion
- metadata completion

### Expand
- course workflow
- event participation
- partner referral
- second title
- derivative asset creation

## Content-level fields

Every major campaign asset should specify:

- audience
- funnel stage
- pitch
- CTA
- proof requirement
- success event
- source/campaign
- owner
- review date

## Proof policy

Customer outcomes require:
- consent
- context
- accurate timing
- exact scope
- no invented causality

## North-star principle

Do not let impressions substitute for completed books.
