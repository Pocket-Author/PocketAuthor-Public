# PocketAuthor Reels / TikTok / Shorts Scripts

## Script 1 — The book hiding in your work

**Hook:** You may already have half a book—and not know it.

**On camera:** Open your notes app, old decks, podcast transcripts, client frameworks, research folders.

**Voiceover:** Most experts do not start from nothing. They start from years of fragments created for different purposes.

**On-screen:** Notes ≠ book. Talks ≠ book. Posts ≠ book.

**Voiceover:** The work is finding the recurring idea, the reader, and the promise that connects them.

**Close:** Stop asking “what should I write?” Ask “what book is already hiding inside my work?”

**CTA:** Explore your Book Opportunity.

---

## Script 2 — Founder archive

**Hook:** A founder’s archive is often an unwritten book.

**Visuals:** decks, product docs, launch notes, customer research, talks.

**Voiceover:** But the startup story itself may not be the book.

The stronger book might be the framework you learned, the market truth you discovered, or the operating principle you can teach.

**Close:** The chronology is source material. The lesson is the book.

---

## Script 3 — AI and authorship

**Hook:** AI can help you write a book. That does not mean AI should become the author.

**Voiceover:** AI can organize, compare, summarize, draft, and edit.

But it cannot take responsibility for your claims, your sources, your permissions, or your judgment.

**On-screen:** Assist ≠ approve.

**Close:** Faster writing should not mean weaker authorship.

---

## Script 4 — Finished draft

**Hook:** “I finished my manuscript” is not the same as “my book is ready to publish.”

**Visual checklist:** edit, sources, permissions, metadata, cover, ISBN, EPUB, print files, proof.

**Voiceover:** Publishing starts where the blank document ends.

**CTA:** Run a publishing-readiness check.

---

## Script 5 — Bestseller myth

**Hook:** Your book does not have to be a bestseller to be valuable.

**Voiceover:** A book can become a workshop, keynote, course, client education system, recruiting asset, community resource, or professional legacy.

**Close:** Sales matter. But they are not the only return.

---

## Script 6 — One manuscript, many editions

**Hook:** Paperback. Ebook. Audiobook. Translation. Same book?

**Voiceover:** They should all trace back to one approved manuscript.

Otherwise every format becomes its own version of the truth.

**On-screen:** One canonical book → many controlled editions.

**CTA:** Build editions without losing the source.
